Running Jupyter Notebook on Bocconi HPC from your laptop

Prerequisites (one-time setup — already done)

- Jupyter Notebook 7.5 is now installed in sam3_venv
- SLURM job script saved at ~/jupyter_gpu.slurm

---
Step 1 — Connect to the HPC login node

On your laptop, connect to the university VPN, then:

ssh 3152128@lnode01.hpc.unibocconi.it

---
Step 2 — Submit the Jupyter GPU job

sbatch ~/jupyter_gpu.slurm

You'll get back a job ID, e.g. Submitted batch job 469500. The job requests:
- 1 GPU (GA100), 4 CPUs, 32 GB RAM
- 8 hours on the gpu partition

---
Step 3 — Wait for the job to start, then read connection info

# Check job status (wait until STATE = R)
squeue -u 3152128

# Once running, read the connection info
cat ~/slogs/jupyter_session.txt

Output will look like:
Node    : gnode01
Port    : 54321
SSH tunnel command (run on your laptop):
    ssh -N -L 54321:gnode01:54321 3152128@lnode01.hpc.unibocconi.it

The authentication token appears in the job log:
cat ~/slogs/jupyter_<JOBID>.out | grep token

---
Step 4 — Open an SSH tunnel from your laptop

Open a new terminal on your laptop (keep the VPN active) and run the tunnel command from Step 3:

ssh -N -L 54321:gnode01:54321 3152128@lnode01.hpc.unibocconi.it

This forwards localhost:54321 on your laptop → through the login node → to Jupyter on the GPU node. Leave this
terminal open.

---
Step 5 — Open Jupyter in your browser

Navigate to:
http://localhost:54321

Paste the token when prompted. You now have a Jupyter Notebook running on a GA100 GPU, accessible from your local
browser.

---
Stopping the session

# From the login node:
scancel <JOBID>

# Or just shut down Jupyter from the browser's File > Shut Down

---
Customizing the job

Edit ~/jupyter_gpu.slurm to change:
